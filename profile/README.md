# Iterant Systems

**Hardware, Evolved.**

Iterant Systems is an Ottawa-based engineering practice focused on precision
electronics, embedded systems, custom PCB development, prototype realization,
hardware integration, and validation.

We support research groups, startups, and advanced product teams through
complete hardware-development programs, individual subsystems, or specific
technical work packages.

## Capabilities

- Precision analog and mixed-signal electronics
- Embedded control and Linux systems
- High-speed and high-density PCB design
- Scientific instrumentation
- Prototype realization and PCBA coordination
- Industrial embedded-computing integration
- Hardware bring-up and debugging
- Automated testing, characterization, and validation

## Engineering Through Realization

Iterant can support projects from requirements and system architecture through:

- Schematic and PCB development
- Embedded firmware and system integration
- Component sourcing and BOM management
- PCB fabrication and assembly coordination
- First-article bring-up
- Test automation and performance validation
- Deployment support and design iteration
